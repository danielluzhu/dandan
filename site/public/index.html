02 — Investing

Where I write
checks.

Early and technical. I'm most useful before product-market fit, when the hard questions are still engineering questions.

◼

AI

Models earning their keep inside a real workflow — not demos. I'm drawn to teams with a data or distribution advantage that compounds, and to the applied layer where accuracy has a dollar value attached.

◆

Hardware

Atoms are unforgiving and that's the moat. I've shipped a physical product and know what a DFM review, a tooling schedule, and a first production run actually cost you. Sensing, devices, instrumentation, advanced manufacturing.

▲

Robotics

Automation pointed at work people genuinely don't want to do. My own first robot picked up dog waste, so I have a high tolerance for unglamorous problems with real customers behind them.

What I look for

  • Technical founders who could build v1 themselves — and did
  • A wedge where the hard part is hard for a reason
  • Earliest rounds: pre-seed and seed
  • Customers who feel the pain in a budget line, not a survey
  • Clear thinking about unit economics before the first unit ships

How I help after the wire

  • Hardware 0→1: prototype to pilot to production, and the traps between
  • Landing enterprise design partners and running pilots that convert
  • Hiring and leveling early engineers — I've sat on both sides
  • Scaling systems before they break, from someone who watched them break
  • Straight answers. I'd rather be useful twice a year than noisy weekly

03 — Background

University of Washington. I came up through circuits, silicon, and computer vision — the stack I now fund.

Poomba Robotics

An autonomous robot that collects dog waste. Navigation, perception, and a mechanism nobody else wanted to design.

Utility-line defouling drone

A UAV built to clear fouling from live utility lines — flight, payload, and a very low appetite for failure.

Pipelined ARM processor

A pipelined ARM core implemented in SystemVerilog. Hazards, forwarding, and the discipline of hardware you can't patch.

Lane detection
