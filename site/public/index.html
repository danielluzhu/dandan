02 — Investing

What I invest in.

Early-stage, and usually technical. I'm most useful before product-market fit, when the open questions are still engineering and go-to-market ones.

◼

AI

Products where a model does real work inside a workflow someone already pays for. I look for a data or distribution advantage that gets stronger over time.

◆

Hardware

Physical products are slow and expensive to get right, which is exactly why they're defensible. Having sold and operated a hardware business, I know what the manufacturing and sales timelines actually look like.

▲

Robotics

Automation aimed at work that's hard to staff. I care most about whether the unit economics hold once you account for service and downtime.

What I look for

  • Technical founders who built the first version themselves
  • A clear reason the hard part is hard
  • Pre-seed and seed rounds
  • Customers with the problem in a budget line, not just a survey
  • Honest thinking about unit economics early

How I can help

  • Selling into enterprises and running pilots that convert
  • Hardware go-to-market: pricing, channel, and deployment
  • Operations and hiring as the team grows past the founders
  • Real estate, leases, and physical footprint questions
  • Straight answers, and an introduction when I actually have one
